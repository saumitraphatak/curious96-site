## Preserved research content

- Hood Lab lithium-cesium program has three milestones:
  1. First optical-tweezer trapping and high-fidelity imaging of a single 6Li atom.
  2. Unified theoretical model for sub-Doppler cooling in tweezers.
  3. Narrow-line electric-quadrupole cooling and background-free imaging for 133Cs.
- Lithium work included dual-species chamber commissioning, 671 nm Zeeman slower, field nulling, gray molasses, single 6Li atom in 1064 nm tweezer, and 2000 images with 99.95% survival.
- Theory work: master-equation model for motional and hyperfine dynamics, covering polarization-gradient, gray-molasses, Lambda-enhanced, and Raman/sideband schemes.
- Cesium work: 6S1/2 to 5D5/2 electric-quadrupole transition, 685 nm, Gamma/2pi about 117 kHz, Laguerre-Gaussian vortex beam, 852 nm cascade fluorescence, magic ellipticity, stretched-state cycle, 99.58% state-detection fidelity, 5 microkelvin in 1.1 mK trap.
- Outlook: deterministic assembly of ground-state LiCs molecules, permanent dipole moment roughly 5.5 Debye.
